# KerimAnka UI 0.35.1 — Classic Era bag improvements

- Three bag designs, improved search/controls, quest-item highlights and multilingual text fixes.
- Reduced unnecessary bag refresh work and reorganized bag settings.
- Optional independent KerimAnka Bags edition, packaged separately with its own settings and media.

Suite users: download **KerimAnkaUI-0.35.1-complete-suite.zip**.
Classic Era bags only: download **KerimAnkaBags-ClassicEra-0.35.1.zip**.
The suite already contains its integrated bag feature; you do not need both downloads.
Independent bags currently supports Classic Era only. Forever behavior is unchanged.
Keep your WTF/SavedVariables when updating. Independent and suite settings remain separate.
