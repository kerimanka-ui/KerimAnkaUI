BlizzardRogueCombo.blp contains original Blizzard Entertainment World of Warcraft interface artwork.
Source: locally installed World of Warcraft Forever 1.60.1.70532, FileDataID 8474117 (Interface/HUD/UIRogueComboPoints2xC60.blp).
Blizzard artwork remains the property of Blizzard Entertainment; it is not original KerimAnka UI artwork and is not covered by the project-owned artwork license.
The Classic-only renderer references the original sprite coordinates and implements its own point display logic.
